About Lyrian

Feel the Music Beyond Hearing

Lyrian deconstructs songs into isolated stems (vocals, drums, bass, and other) using state-of-the-art AI source separation models. It translates rhythm and frequencies into haptic vibrations and reactive audio-visualizers, making music an accessible, deeply immersive sensory journey.

Help & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my audio kept private?
Yes. Your audio files are processed strictly for playback, stem extraction, and sensory rendering. Your personal files are never sold, transmitted for advertising, or used to train third-party models.
What audio files can I use?
You can load your own DRM-free audio files (MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC), record directly through the microphone, or explore our built-in royalty-free demo tracks right away.
