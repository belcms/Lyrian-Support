1. About the Application

Lyrian offers audio playback, file importation, microphone recording, AI-driven musical component separation (stems), reactive visuals, and synchronized haptic vibration responses. Audio recording is initiated actively by the user. In Live mode, the microphone captures ambient sound to generate immediate visuals, haptics, and song recognition without permanently storing the audio session.
